## What we collect

**When you place an order:** name, email, phone number, billing and delivery address, and what you bought.

**When you create an account:** the above, plus your password in hashed form. We never store it in readable text.

**When you pay:** our payment provider handles your card details directly. We receive only a confirmation, the last four digits, and the card type. Full card numbers never reach our servers.

**When you contact us:** whatever you write, so we can answer and keep a record of the conversation.

**When you use the site:** IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, and referring site. This is technical data used to keep the site working and secure.

**When you subscribe:** your email address, and nothing else.

| Marketing emails | Your consent |
| Improving the site | Legitimate interest |

We do not use your data to make automated decisions that produce legal effects for you.

## Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary, and only with:

- **Payment providers**, to take payment
- **Shipping carriers**, to deliver your order
- **Email and hosting providers**, to run the site and send order notifications
- **SendFox**, to collect newsletter subscriptions and send the marketing emails you request, including subscription confirmation and unsubscribe handling
- **Accountants and tax authorities**, where the law requires it

Each of these is bound to use the data only for that purpose.

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.**

If our business is ever sold or merged, your data may transfer to the new owner, who would remain bound by this policy.

## Cookies

We use cookies for three things:

**Essential** — keeping you signed in and remembering your basket. The site cannot work without these.

**Preferences** — remembering choices like language or currency.

**Analytics** — understanding which pages are used, in aggregate. Only with your consent.

You can refuse non-essential cookies through the banner or your browser settings. Refusing them does not stop you shopping.
